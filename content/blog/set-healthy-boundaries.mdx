---
title: "Setting healthy boundaries on hybrid connections"
description: "A simple framework to state your limits and invite others to do the same."
date: "2023-12-18"
locale: "en"
---

Boundaries are invitations to connect with clarity. Share how you prefer to communicate, when you are available and what topics are off limits.

> Tip: Write a short paragraph in your profile about how you ask for consent before sharing personal details.

If a conversation feels uncomfortable, use the pause conversation feature and let the other person know you will revisit it later.
