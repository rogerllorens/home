---
title: "Joining interest groups that feel safe"
description: "Practical guidance to choose moderated spaces and add value to every meetup."
date: "2024-02-05"
locale: "en"
---

Groups are the heart of Xder. Before joining, read the group rules, introduce yourself briefly and ask how you can help the gathering run smoothly.

- Signal your accessibility needs.
- Offer to support the host with check-in reminders.
- Share feedback after every activity.

When the energy feels low, enable low interaction mode so others know you prefer listening over leading the conversation.
