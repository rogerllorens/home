# GSC Opportunity Engine

GSC opportunities are merged with internal quality opportunities. Types include high impressions with low CTR, quick wins in positions 4-15, positions 16-30 content gaps, impressions with no clicks, query not in meta/title, low internal quality with demand and approved versions not exported with real impressions.

Scores combine demand volume, position opportunity, CTR gap, quality gap, match confidence and actionability. These are Rankelia heuristics, not Google recommendations, and do not guarantee ranking changes.
