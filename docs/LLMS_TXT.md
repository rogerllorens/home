# llms.txt Generator

Rankelia can generate a simple `llms.txt` proposal from the free audit. The format is emerging and not an official guarantee of inclusion in AI systems.

## What it includes

- Home URL.
- Sitemap and robots.txt if detected.
- Platform hint if detected.
- Placeholders for categories, products, guides, contact and policies that must be reviewed manually.
- Disclaimer that prices, stock and conditions must be checked on original pages.

## How to use

Download or copy the generated TXT, review it and upload it manually to the root of the domain as `/llms.txt` if you want to test it.

## Limitations

Rankelia does not invent URLs, products, categories, prices, stock or claims. A generated file does not guarantee visibility in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, AI Overviews or other AI systems.
